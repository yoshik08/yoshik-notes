## Direction contract

THESIS: Your notes are a contact sheet of handwritten frames on a true-black sheet: picking one is a red grease-pencil circle, opening one is the enlarger. Refuses the iPad-mockup split hero over a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Total black ground. Frames are the notes' own pages (paper white or black) at 3:2, separated by hairline rebates, with tabular edge-print numerals in condensed Archivo under each frame. Display in Archivo expanded, interface in Geist. One accent, grease-pencil red, used only for marks: selection, the live frame, focus. White is reserved for ink, content, and the primary action.

STORY: A visitor sees real handwriting laid out as a contact sheet, writes in the circled live frame with a pencil, finger, or mouse, understands this is a browser notebook made for Apple Pencil, and signs in with Google to keep it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The contact sheet is the page. A strip of example frames (labelled as examples) runs across the top; the live frame sits enlarged at the left, circled in red, writable immediately; the headline is set large in the sheet's margin beside it, with the "Continue with Google" white pill directly under the headline. The wordmark sits top-left in the edge print.
